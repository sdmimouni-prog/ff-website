MY BIOGRAPHY

An artist inspired
by people

Originally from Tetouan, Farah El Fassi first gained recognition in cinema with Le Temps des camarades by Mohamed Chrif Tribak, presented in 2008. She reunited with the director for Petits bonheurs, then worked with Mohamed Ismaïl on Dés… Espoirs and La Mora.

Her career spans film and television: La Première Page, Salamate et ses filles, Bayn Al Qosour and Rahma. Her film appearances also include Quitte ou double by Rachida Saadi and L’Batal by Omar Lotfi.

LEARN MORE

After earning her first baccalaureate in 2012 and studying at the National Institute of Fine Arts in Tetouan, she returned to her studies and earned another baccalaureate in 2021. Her career was honoured with a tribute at the Salé International Women’s Film Festival in 2025.Sources: Wikipedia · Salé Film Festival · Le360

MY VALUES

Commitment
beyond the screen

Education, women’s rights,
culture for everyone.

I believe in the power of culture to bring people together, encourage dialogue and build a more caring society. Through my artistic choices and commitments, I want to shine a light on stories that matter and support causes close to my heart, including education, women’s rights and access to culture for everyone.
